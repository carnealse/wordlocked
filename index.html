HOW TO PLAY

Crack 3 combination locks: each lock gives you 5 guesses.

  1. Three locks to crack: 4 → 5 → 6 letters.
  2. 5 tries per lock.
  3. Spin the dials using the < > arrows, swipe, or keyboard arrows.
  4. Press LOCK IT IN to submit your guess.
  5. A green dial means that is a correct letter.
  6. Get all dials green to crack the lock and advance.

New puzzle drops every day at midnight Eastern Time.
Everyone worldwide gets the same locks.
