HOW TO PLAY

Crack 3 combination locks using only 5 total guesses.

1 Each lock is a word: 4 letters → 5 letters → 6 letters.
2 Spin the dials to set your guess. Use the ▲ ▼ arrows, swipe up/down on a dial, or type on a keyboard.
3 Hit TRY THE LOCK to submit.
4 A green dial means that letter is correct and in the right position.
5 Get all dials green to crack the lock and advance to the next.

A new puzzle drops every day at midnight UTC. Everyone gets the same locks.
